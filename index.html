🩺

Leading Through Clinical Mastery, Empathy, and Uncompromising Standards

For more than two decades in nursing education and clinical healthcare administration, Mr. Manoj L. Deshmukh has dedicated his career to the noble mission of healthcare accessibility and academic excellence. As Principal cum Professor of Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna—established under the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra—he leads the institutional charge in preparing compassionate, competent, and courageous nurses who will shape the future of healthcare.

Holding an advanced specialization in Medical-Surgical Nursing with a dedicated focus on Oncology, Principal Deshmukh harmonizes clinical acumen with profound humanitarian devotion. Under his vision, the college ensures that quality nursing education reaches students from all sections of society, especially from rural and underserved communities across Maharashtra—transforming students into skilled, ethical healthcare professionals who uphold the highest standards of patient care.

SECTION V • CHRONICLE OF MILESTONES

The Chronicle of Institutional Genesis

A historical timeline charting the statutory approvals, campus establishment, and inaugural milestones of Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna.

2023
GOVERNMENT MANDATE EPOCH I

State Sanction & Public Health Dept. Approval

Government of Maharashtra sanctioned the establishment of Government B.Sc. Nursing College at Jalna under the Public Health Department to expand specialized healthcare education in the Marathwada region.

2024
UNIVERSITY AFFILIATION EPOCH II

MUHS Nashik Affiliation & Council Approvals

Secured formal statutory affiliation with Maharashtra University of Health Sciences, Nashik (College Code: 154153), alongside approvals from the Indian Nursing Council (INC New Delhi) and Maharashtra Nursing Council (MNC Mumbai).

2024
LEADERSHIP INVESTITURE EPOCH III

Appointment of Mr. Manoj L. Deshmukh as Principal

Mr. Manoj L. Deshmukh, M.Sc. Medical Surgical Nursing (Oncology), assumed charge as Principal cum Professor, laying the institutional foundation, faculty recruitment, and advanced simulation laboratory architecture.

2024–25
INAUGURAL BATCH EPOCH IV

Inaugural 50-Seat B.Sc. Nursing Batch & Hospital Immersion

Welcomed the maiden cohort of 50 student nurses through the Maharashtra State CET Cell (College Code: 09385), inaugurating full clinical rotations at the District Civil Hospital Campus, Jalna.

From the Principal's Desk

Official Executive Address to Students, Faculty, Parents & Healthcare Community • Academic Sessions 2024–2026

Dear Students, Esteemed Faculty, Cherished Parents, and Healthcare Colleagues,

"Education is the most powerful instrument we have to prepare compassionate, competent and courageous nurses who will shape the future of healthcare."

It is my privilege and honour to welcome you to the Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna — a premier institution established under the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra, and affiliated to Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik.

Our college was established with a clear and noble mission: to provide quality nursing education to students from all sections of society, especially from rural and underserved communities. We believe that every student who walks through our doors carries the potential to transform lives — of patients, families and communities.

At Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna, we are committed to building an environment of academic excellence, clinical competence and strong professional values. Our dedicated faculty, well-equipped skills laboratory, strong hospital affiliations and vibrant student activities ensure that our students receive a holistic, well-rounded education that prepares them not just for examinations, but for the realities and responsibilities of nursing practice.

Nursing is not merely a profession; it is a sacred trust. It demands knowledge, skill, discipline, and above all, deep human empathy. As our students learn the science of healing and the art of caregiving, we strive to instill in them the ethics, resilience, and compassion that define a truly great healthcare professional.

To our faculty, staff and all stakeholders — your dedication, guidance and support are the foundation upon which our students build their futures. Together, we are building an institution of excellence, integrity and compassion.

"Let us walk together on this path of knowledge, service and transformation."

With warm regards and best wishes,

Manoj L. Deshmukh
